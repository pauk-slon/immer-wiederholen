# Lesson recall guide

## The task

The learner translates sentences from their language into German.

- Write two or three sentences. Use one exercise in each sentence. Use two exercises in one sentence only if they fit one situation by themselves.
- A natural sentence matters more than using every exercise. Leave out an exercise that doesn't fit an everyday sentence.
- Use first the exercises that the learner answered wrong in the lesson.
- Write the sentences in the learner's language, never in German.
- The reference translation is correct, standard German. It uses the form that the exercise practices.

## The topics

- `partizip_ii`: the Partizip II of a verb. A sentence in the Perfekt with this verb.
- `partizip_ii_meaning`: the Partizip II in a sentence. A sentence in the Perfekt with this verb.
- `perfekt_wortstellung`: the word order of the Perfekt. A sentence that starts with a time or a place, so the subject comes after the auxiliary verb.
- `verb_preposition`: a verb with its preposition and case, such as `warten auf` + Akkusativ.
- `preposition_meaning`: the meaning of a preposition, such as `auf` + Dativ for a place or `seit` for a duration.

## The check

A translation is correct if it is correct German and keeps the meaning of the sentence.

- Other words or another word order are correct, if the German is correct and the meaning is the same.
- The practiced form is never required. A correct translation without it is correct.
- Spoken German and common variants are correct.
- A typo that doesn't change a grammatical form doesn't make the translation wrong.
- A grammar mistake makes the translation wrong. Examples are a wrong case, ending, article, verb form, word order or preposition.
- A changed meaning or a missing part makes the translation wrong.

## The explanation

Write the explanation in the learner's language. In Russian, address the learner as "вы".

- If the translation has no mistake, give no explanation.
- Otherwise name each mistake and its fix, in one or two short sentences. Start with the practiced form.
- If the learner translated around the practiced form in correct German, say that the translation is correct and name the form that the lesson practiced.
- No praise and no summary.
